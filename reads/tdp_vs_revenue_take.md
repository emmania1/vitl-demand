# TDP Growth vs Revenue Growth · 2026-05-20

Management claims TDPs (total distribution points — shelf SKU placements)
grew ~20% in Q1 2026 while revenue grew 15.4%. Over the long run these
two should converge. If TDPs keep outpacing revenue, velocity per shelf
slot is broken (a price-or-promotion problem). If they reconverge,
recovery is real and the gap was just timing.
