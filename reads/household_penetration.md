# Household Penetration · 2025-12-31

**14.2M households** at year-end 2025. **+2.0M YoY** — net new buyers
despite the cycle entering disruption mid-year. Q1 2026 figure pending
the next call disclosure.

**Why it matters:** household penetration is the leading indicator for
whether the funnel is still pulling in new buyers. Over 500K quarterly
growth = funnel healthy. Under 500K = slow-bleed concern. A decline
would be the first hard data that the brand controversy / price gap
combination is denting acquisition.
