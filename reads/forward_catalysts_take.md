resolution (~June)**, **Q2 FY26 print (Aug 6)**, and the **FY26 print +
FY27 guide (~Feb 2027)**. The first is binary (clean amendment = floor;

easy comps. With Q4 2026 being the first easy comp, the window
**Aug-Oct 2026** is where the recovery story can start to take shape
in the tape — assuming covenants resolve cleanly and Q2 doesn't get
worse than guided.
